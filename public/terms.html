About Fleet OPS

Fleet OPS is fleet compliance and maintenance software for lorry, trailer, and bus operators. It can help customers organize vehicle papers, expiry status and reminders, document holds, trip records, fuel, driver accounts, maintenance, tyres, and related reports. The service is not a government filing service, accounting package, ticketing system, GPS or live-tracking service, driver-monitoring system, or driver app.

Customer responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information and keep records up to date.
  • Manage authorized users and protect their account credentials.
  • Ensure they have the rights and permissions needed to enter vehicle, driver, employee, and trip information.
  • Review records, reminders, and documents and independently confirm legal and operational requirements.

Not legal or compliance advice

Fleet OPS provides recordkeeping and reminder tools. Customers remain responsible for renewals, required documents, and decisions about whether a vehicle may operate.
