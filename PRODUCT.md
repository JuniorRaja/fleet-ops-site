# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Fleet owners, transport in-charges, and accountants at truck, bus, and logistics operators in India. They manage vehicle papers, dispatch decisions, workshop work, and trip records from an office or yard, often with a phone close at hand.

## Product Purpose

Fleet OPS is compliance and maintenance software for fleet operators. It helps teams keep six vehicle papers current, receive lead-time warnings, hold unfit vehicles at dispatch, and maintain accountable workshop and trip records. The site explains the product and invites visitors to book a demo. Fleet OPS is not GPS tracking or driver surveillance software.

## Brand Personality

Practical, candid, grounded. The voice should feel like a competent transport in-charge: direct about operational risks, clear about what the product does, and honest about what it does not do.

## Anti-references

- Do not present Fleet OPS as a GPS tracker, live-map product, or driver-surveillance system.
- Do not use generic SaaS claims, fabricated outcomes, or feature lists detached from fleet work.
- Do not obscure product limits, pricing conditions, or the next step to contact the team.
- Avoid template-like marketing layouts that could belong to any software company.

## Design Principles

- Show the paper, dispatch, and workshop realities that operators recognize.
- Connect each feature to a concrete operational consequence.
- Make exceptions accountable: name, time, and reason matter.
- Be explicit that Fleet OPS does not track vehicles.
- Keep the demo request clear and reachable on phones as well as desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep all actions keyboard-accessible with visible focus, use text as well as color to communicate status, maintain readable contrast, and honor reduced-motion preferences. Mobile layouts should remain usable in a yard or office with touch input.
