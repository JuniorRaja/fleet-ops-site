# Remaining Website Roadmap

Updated: 2 November 2026

## Before launch

1. Confirm claims about exports, reminders, response times, and compliance workflows.
2. Confirm the ten-year experience and data protection statements.
3. Test touch, sound, keyboard use, and performance on physical mobile devices.
4. Validate structured data after deployment using public search tools.
5. Get professional review for the privacy policy and service terms.

## Analytics after launch

1. Done: demo actions and trip-sheet interactions are tracked. Events are listed in `src/lib/analytics.ts` call sites and `data-umami-event` attributes.
2. Review Umami results after two weeks of representative traffic.
3. Remove events that do not support a clear business decision.

## Content waiting for approval

1. Add more testimonials only after receiving approved customer statements.
2. Add manufacturer references only when wording avoids implied endorsements.

## Exit rule

Launch when mobile checks, legal review, and public validation are complete.
